My Favourite Animal

Dogs are friendly and loyal animals.
They are intelligent and playful.
Dogs make wonderful pets.

  • Habitat: Dogs live with humans in homes and can also live in shelters or farms.
  • Diet: Dogs are omnivores and eat a mixture of meat, vegetables, grains, and specially prepared dog food.
  • Lifespan: Most dogs live for around 10-13 years, although this varies by breed and size.
